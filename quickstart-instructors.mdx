---
title: "Get Started with Learning In Stride as an Instructor"
description: "Sign up for an instructor account, complete billing, run the Course Setup Wizard, and share enrollment tokens so students can join your course."
---

Learning In Stride gives RPI ITWS instructors a unified Hub for managing courses, rosters, grading, and scheduling. This guide covers the instructor-specific signup flow, how to build your first course with the Course Setup Wizard, and how to invite students using enrollment tokens.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an instructor account">
    Instructor accounts use a separate signup page from regular student registration. Navigate to `/professor-signup` (or follow the link from the pricing page) to begin.

    Select a plan before you reach the form. Available plans are:

    | Plan | Monthly price |
    |------|--------------|
    | Basic | $29/mo |
    | Pro | $59/mo |
    | Premium | $99/mo |
    | Platinum | $149/mo |
    | Founding Instructor | $49/mo |

    All plans include a **14-day free trial**. Annual billing saves an additional 15% on any plan.

    <Note>
      If you sign up with an `@rpi.edu` email address, a 15% academic discount is applied to your subscription automatically.
    </Note>

    Fill in your full name, institutional email, institution name (optional), and a password of at least 8 characters. Choose monthly or annual billing, then click **Continue to Payment**. You will be redirected to Stripe Checkout to complete the transaction. After payment, you land on the onboarding screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete account verification">
    After payment, Learning In Stride redirects you to `/onboarding` where you link your instructor account to your RPI identity.

    - **RIN** (required) — your 9-digit RPI Identification Number.
    - **RCS ID** (required) — your RCS username (e.g., `plotkr`). If you used RPI Shibboleth to sign up, this field is pre-verified and locked.
    - **Preferred name**, **pronouns**, **Discord username**, and **GitHub username** are optional but recommended.

    Click **Complete Setup**. Instructors with the `PROFESSOR` role are redirected to the Staff Dashboard at `/admin/staff` rather than the student dashboard.

    <Tip>
      Connect your Discord account during onboarding. The enrollment token flow grants students access to your course's Discord server automatically, so your Discord server must be configured before you distribute tokens.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run the Course Setup Wizard">
    From the Staff Dashboard, open the **Course Setup Wizard** (`/admin/course-setup-wizard`). The wizard walks you through six sequential steps to get your course ready for the semester:

    1. **Academic Calendar** — loads semester dates (classes start/end, finals start/end) directly from the RPI academic calendar. Confirm or adjust the parsed dates.
    2. **Select Courses** — choose the course or courses you are teaching this semester from the course catalog. Cross-listed sections can be grouped into a single course card.
    3. **Build Schedule** — copy the class schedule from a previous semester or build one from scratch. Each week's meeting pattern and class days are populated automatically from the RPI class hour schedule.
    4. **Import Students** — upload your SIS roster file and optional student photos. Learning In Stride parses the roster and pre-populates your enrollment list.
    5. **Publish Course** — flip the course to visible so students can see it on their dashboards and in enrollment links. You can keep the course hidden while still generating enrollment tokens.
    6. **Course Tools** — access schedule management, grading configuration, and enrollment tools from a single panel.

    Each step shows a progress indicator. You can return to any completed step to make changes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite students with enrollment tokens">
    Go to the **Hub** (`/hub`) or the enrollment section of Course Tools. Generate an enrollment token for your course. Each token is tied to a specific course and semester.

    Share the generated link (or QR code) with your students. When a student opens the link:

    1. Learning In Stride validates the token and displays your course code, name, semester, and your name.
    2. The student connects their Discord account.
    3. The platform adds them to the class Discord server, assigns the course role, and optionally assigns a team role.
    4. Their profile information is saved and linked to your course roster.

    <Warning>
      Enrollment tokens are public URLs. Anyone with the link can attempt to enroll. Students whose RIN does not match the imported roster are placed in a pending state and require your manual approval before they can access course content.
    </Warning>

    You can view pending approvals, active enrollments, and enrollment activity from the Hub at any time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Manage your courses from the Hub">
    The Staff Dashboard at `/admin/staff` is your day-to-day command center. It shows:

    - **Course filter pills** at the top — quickly scope all widgets to a single course or view all courses at once. Cross-listed sections appear as a combined pill (e.g., `ITWS 4100 / 6800`).
    - **Stat cards** — total enrolled students, pending approvals, average grade, and upcoming due dates, all scoped to your selected course.
    - **Teaching Agenda** — your upcoming class sessions pulled from Schedule Master.
    - **Grading Queue** — assignments awaiting grading, sorted by due date.
    - **At-Risk panel** — students whose grade or engagement signals suggest they need outreach.
    - **Recent Activity** — a feed of submissions, enrollments, and grade events.

    Each course card links out directly to the dedicated editor for that data: assignments open the assignment manager, grades open the grading roster, schedule opens the Schedule Center.
  </Step>
</Steps>
