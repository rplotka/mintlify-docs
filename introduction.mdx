Learning In Stride is a modern Learning Management System built for RPI ITWS courses. Whether you're a student tracking assignments and grades or an instructor managing coursework, schedules, and grading workflows, this platform brings everything into one place — including AI-assisted grading, Discord integration, exam preparation, and gamification.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Visit the Learning In Stride sign-up page and register with your RPI email or Google account. Instructors can sign up at the **Professor Sign-Up** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enroll in or create a course">
    Students use an enrollment token from their instructor to join a course. Instructors use the Course Setup Wizard to create and configure their course.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your dashboard">
    Once enrolled, your personalized dashboard shows upcoming assignments, schedule, grades, and any announcements from your courses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore features">
    Connect Discord, enable gamification, prepare for exams, and customize your notification preferences from **Settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
