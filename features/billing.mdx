## Managing your subscription

### Billing Dashboard

Navigate to **Billing** from your account menu to reach the Billing Dashboard. The dashboard shows:

- Your current plan name and monthly price
- Subscription status (active, trialing, or past due)
- Course and student-per-course limits
- Whether participation analytics is included
- Your current AI add-on tier (if any)
- TA seats allocated to your account
- Next billing date
- Any active discount codes

### Upgrading or downgrading

Click **Manage in Stripe** from the Billing Dashboard to open the Stripe customer portal. From there you can:

- Switch to a higher or lower plan
- Add, change, or remove an AI add-on
- Switch between monthly and annual billing
- Apply a coupon code

Plan changes take effect at the start of your next billing period unless you are upgrading, in which case the change applies immediately and Stripe prorates the difference.

### Canceling your subscription

Cancel from the Stripe customer portal at any time. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. After that, your courses remain readable but you cannot create new courses, enroll new students, or use paid features.

<Warning>
  Canceling does not delete your data. Course content, grades, and student records are preserved. You can reactivate your subscription at any time to regain full access.
</Warning>

### Past-due payments

If a payment fails, your subscription enters a **Past Due** state and a banner appears at the top of the Billing Dashboard. Click **Update Payment Method** in the banner to open the Stripe portal and resolve the issue before service is interrupted.

## Usage limits

Each plan enforces two hard limits: the number of active courses and the number of enrolled students per course. If you reach a limit, you will not be able to create additional courses or enroll additional students until you upgrade.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Course limit reached" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    You cannot create new courses. Archive or delete an existing course, or upgrade to a plan with more courses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Student limit reached" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    New students cannot be enrolled in the affected course. Upgrade to a plan with a higher per-course student cap.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

AI action limits reset on your monthly billing anniversary. Plan and student limits are enforced continuously.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I start without a credit card?">
    Yes. The 14-day free trial requires no payment information. You will be prompted to add a payment method before the trial ends if you want to continue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What counts as an 'active course'?">
    Any course semester with at least one enrolled student counts against your course limit. Archived or unpublished courses do not count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple AI add-on tiers at once?">
    No. You can have one AI tier active at a time — either one included with your plan or a separately purchased add-on. Changing tiers replaces the current one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does annual billing lock me in for the full year?">
    Annual billing commits you to 12 months of service billed upfront. You can cancel to prevent renewal, but refunds for unused months are not provided unless required by applicable law.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get an invoice for my records?">
    Past invoices are listed on the Billing Dashboard in the **Invoice History** section. Each invoice links to a downloadable PDF via Stripe.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
