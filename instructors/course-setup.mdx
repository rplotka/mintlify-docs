The Course Setup Wizard takes you from a blank semester to a fully published course in six steps. You work through each step in order, but you can return to any completed step at any time from the wizard's progress bar.

## Starting the wizard

Navigate to **Admin** and click **Course Setup** in the sidebar. The wizard opens to step one automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Load the academic calendar">
    Learning In Stride pulls semester dates directly from the RPI academic calendar. When the wizard opens, it auto-discovers available semesters and shows which ones are already loaded.

    - Semesters marked **Loaded** already exist in the system — you can skip those.
    - Semesters marked **Ready** have complete class-start and finals-end dates and are ready to create.
    - Semesters marked **Missing dates** could not extract the required dates from the calendar source.

    Select the checkboxes next to any new semesters you want to create, then click **Create Semesters**. Click **Refresh** if you expect a newer calendar to have appeared since the page loaded.

    You need at least one semester before you can continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select your courses">
    The wizard queries RPI SIS using your faculty account to discover courses you are assigned to teach. Courses are grouped by term (Summer, Fall, Spring).

    - Courses marked **Loaded** are already in Learning In Stride — no action needed.
    - Courses marked **New** are available to import.

    Select the courses you want to add and click **Import**. The system links each course to the correct semester automatically. Click **Refresh** next to any term if the list looks incomplete.

    <Note>
      If a course is missing from SIS discovery, contact your registrar to confirm your assignment is recorded. You cannot import a course that is not assigned to you in SIS.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build your schedule">
    Choose a course from the dropdown, then pick one of two options:

    **Copy from a previous semester** — select a prior semester from the list. Each option shows the number of weeks and schedule items it contains. Click **Copy & Adjust Dates** and the system re-anchors all item dates to match the new semester's calendar.

    **Edit schedule manually** — click **Open Schedule Editor** to open the full Schedule Editor in a new tab. See [Schedule Center](/instructors/schedule) for instructions on adding lectures, labs, and office hours.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Import students">
    Select the course you want to populate, then upload one or both of the following:

    **SIS Roster (Excel or CSV)** — export the roster by CRN from Banner/SIS. Required columns are `email`, `name`, `rcs_id`, and `student_id`. The `section` column is optional.

    **Student photos (ZIP)** — upload a ZIP file of student headshots. The system matches photos to roster entries by RCS ID and displays them on the enrollment list.

    After uploading each file, a confirmation toast shows how many students were imported or matched.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish the course">
    A course in **Draft** state is invisible to students. When you are ready, click **Publish** on this step. Students who are enrolled will immediately gain access to the course in their Hub.

    You can unpublish a course at any time from **Admin → Courses** if you need to make the course private again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Access course tools">
    The final step is a quick-launch panel. From here you can jump directly to:

    - **Schedule** — add or edit class meetings
    - **Assignments** — create and manage assignments
    - **Grades** — view the student grade roster
    - **Enrollment** — manage the roster after publishing
    - **Discord** — link a server for course communication

    These same tools are always accessible from the course detail page in **Admin → Courses**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing enrollment after setup

Open **Admin → Courses**, select your course, and click **Enrollment**. From this page you can:

- **Add a user manually** — search by name, email, or RCS ID and assign a role (Instructor, TA, Grader, or Student).
- **Import from file** — upload a Banner/SIS Excel or CSV to add students in bulk, or import a team-assignment sheet (columns: `team`, `email`).
- **Edit an enrollment** — change a student's section, cohort, or extra-time multiplier.
- **Remove a user** — click the trash icon next to any enrollment to unenroll that person.

<Tip>
  Use the **Import** flow whenever you receive an updated roster mid-semester. The importer skips students who are already enrolled, so you can safely re-import without creating duplicates.
</Tip>

## Configuring Discord

If your course uses a Discord server, go to **Admin → Discord** and link the server. Once linked, course announcements and grade notifications can be routed to specific channels. Discord participation data also flows into the Participation Grading service automatically.

## The Professor Dashboard

After setup, your main command center is the **Professor Dashboard** at **Admin → Dashboard**. It shows:

- A summary of courses, students, pending grading items, and average grade for the current semester
- Per-course cards sorted by grading urgency
- AI Insights — at-risk students flagged by the AI risk assessment engine
- Upcoming deadlines across all courses
- Quick links to Settings, Discord, AI, and Export tools

Use the semester selector in the top-right corner to switch the dashboard view between terms.
