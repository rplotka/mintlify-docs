When you log in to Learning In Stride, your dashboard is the first thing you see — and it's designed to show you everything that matters at a glance. From upcoming assignment deadlines and today's schedule to your current grade standing and recent achievements, the dashboard brings together data from all your enrolled courses so you don't have to hunt for it.

## The stats overview

At the top of the dashboard, the **Stats Overview** bar surfaces your most important numbers. These metrics update in real time each time you refresh and include:

- **Assignments due** — how many assignments are pending across all your courses
- **Upcoming events** — class sessions and deadlines in the next seven days
- **Current points** — your total gamification points earned this semester
- **Rank** — your leaderboard position relative to classmates

<Tip>
  Click the **Refresh** button in the header (it shows the time of the last update) to pull the latest data any time you suspect something has changed.
</Tip>

## Upcoming schedule and assignments

Below the stats bar, the dashboard splits into two panels side by side.

**What's Coming Up** is a day-grouped agenda that lists scheduled class sessions, quizzes with open/close windows, and assignment deadlines. Events are grouped by date so you can plan your week at a glance.

**All Assignments** is a filterable list of every assignment across your enrolled courses. Assignments are sorted by urgency:

1. **Late** — past due and not yet submitted (shown in red)
2. **Due** — due within the next seven days (shown in blue)
3. **Scheduled** — due later (shown in gray)
4. **Submitted** — turned in, awaiting a grade (shown in yellow)
5. **Graded** — returned with a score (shown in green)

Quizzes with an active time window are pinned to the top of the list and pulse with a color-coded border to indicate urgency: green for upcoming, yellow for opening within 48 hours, orange for live now, and red for closed.

Use the filter pills at the top of the panel to narrow the list by status or by assignment category. If you have multiple courses, switch between them with the course tabs.

## My courses

The **My Courses** section in the main content area shows a consolidated card for each of your enrolled courses. Each card displays your current progress, grade components, and participation data so you can quickly spot which course needs the most attention this week.

## Content recommendations and study guides

The right sidebar contains a **Recommendations** panel with up to three content suggestions personalized to your performance and upcoming assessments. Below it, the **Study Guides** card links to your personalized study materials for each course, plus a shortcut to **Ask for Help** if you need it.

## Subscribing your calendar

Learning In Stride can push your course schedule — class sessions, assignment deadlines, and exam dates — directly into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, or any calendar that accepts iCal feeds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the calendar button">
    In the top-right header area, click the calendar icon button labeled with your course name. This is the **Calendar Subscription** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or open the feed URL">
    A link to an `.ics` feed is shown. Click **Subscribe** to open your default calendar app, or copy the URL to paste it into any calendar client manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the subscription">
    Accept the subscription in your calendar app. Events will appear and stay in sync automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The calendar feed is read-only. Changes made in your calendar app will not affect Learning In Stride.
</Note>

## Submitting an assignment from the dashboard

You can submit an assignment without navigating away from the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Submit">
    Click the **Submit** button in the top-right header. A modal opens and loads all assignments awaiting submission across every course you're enrolled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the assignment">
    The list sorts late submissions to the top, followed by assignments closest to their due date. Assignments already submitted or graded do not appear. Click the assignment you want to submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your work">
    You are taken directly to that assignment's submission page. Upload your file and confirm the submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If the **Submit** modal shows "No assignments awaiting submission," all your current work is either already turned in or has no submission requirement.
</Tip>

## Checking in to class

If your instructor has started an attendance session, a **Check-in** button appears in the header. The dashboard polls for active sessions every 15 seconds. When a session is live, click **Check-in** to mark your attendance for that class.

## Achievements summary

At the bottom of the dashboard, the **Achievements & Rewards** card shows a compact summary of your recent badges and your current streak. It is collapsed by default — click anywhere on the card to expand it and see your three most recently earned achievements. Click **View all** to go to the full Growth Journey page.

## Mobile navigation

On small screens, the top navigation collapses and a bottom navigation bar appears with quick links to your dashboard, assignments, grades, and settings. All dashboard functionality is available on mobile; the layout stacks into a single column.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does my stats bar show different numbers than the assignment list?">
    The stats bar counts all assignments with a due date, including those without a submission requirement (such as in-class activities). The assignment list only shows items that require a file upload, so the numbers may differ.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The dashboard shows a loading spinner but never finishes. What do I do?">
    Click the **Try Again** button that appears after a failed load. If the problem persists, try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac) or clear your browser cache.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm both a student and a teaching assistant. Which dashboard do I see?">
    If you have both roles, the dashboard defaults to the student view. A **Staff View** button appears in the header so you can switch to the instructor hub at any time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
