Learning In Stride includes a dedicated Series 7 exam preparation module for ITWS courses that incorporate FINRA licensing content. The module guides you through a structured path: start with a diagnostic assessment to map your baseline knowledge, receive a personalized study plan, track your mastery across all 12 Series 7 topic domains, and practice with timed exams drawn from a question bank — all within the same platform you use for coursework.

## Getting started

Navigate to **Exam Prep** from the main sidebar. The dashboard shows three summary cards — your readiness score, the number of exams taken, and your current study phase — along with a call-to-action to take the diagnostic assessment.

<Info>
  The Series 7 is the FINRA General Securities Representative Qualification Exam. A score of 72% or higher is required to pass.
</Info>

## Step 1: Take the diagnostic assessment

The diagnostic assessment is your first action. It asks one question per topic domain to establish your starting knowledge level across all 12 areas. There is no time limit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Take Diagnostic Assessment">
    On the Exam Prep dashboard, click **Take Diagnostic Assessment** in the blue call-to-action card. If you don't have a study plan yet, one is created automatically with a default of 75 study minutes per day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer one question per topic">
    Work through the assessment at your own pace. Each question maps to one of the 12 Series 7 topic domains. The diagnostic takes approximately 15 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your competency profile">
    After completing the diagnostic, your **Initial Competency Profile** appears on the dashboard. It shows your score for each topic domain, color-coded green (72%+), yellow (50–71%), or red (below 50%).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you close the browser mid-diagnostic, the session is saved. Return to the Exam Prep dashboard and click **Resume Diagnostic** to pick up where you left off.
</Tip>

## Your study plan and phases

Once the diagnostic is complete, your study plan moves through four phases as your competency index rises:

| Phase | Label | What's happening |
|-------|-------|-----------------|
| Diagnostic | Getting Started | Identifying your baseline |
| Learning | Building Knowledge | Expanding topic coverage |
| Review | Reinforcing Concepts | Deepening understanding |
| Final Prep | Exam Ready | Final polish before test day |

Your current phase is shown on the Exam Prep dashboard and on the Study Dashboard. Click **View Full Plan** to open the Study Dashboard.

## The Study Dashboard

The Study Dashboard is your daily command center for Series 7 preparation.

### Summary cards

Three cards at the top of the page give you an instant status check:

- **Competency Index** — a circular gauge showing your readiness score out of 100, with a trend arrow indicating whether it is improving, declining, or stable. You need 72 to pass.
- **Study Streak** — how many consecutive days you have completed at least one study task. Each day in the streak is shown as a filled star.
- **Today's Progress** — how many of today's tasks are done and approximately how many minutes of study remain.

### Today's study focus

Your daily tasks are organized into three priority tiers:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Primary — Do First">
    The single most important task for the day, highlighted in blue. This is typically the topic where you have the most to gain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secondary — Do Next">
    Up to two additional tasks to complete after your primary task.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintenance — Keep Sharp">
    Review tasks on topics you've already studied to prevent forgetting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Check off each task by clicking the checkbox on its card. Task types include:

- **Read** — review study material
- **Practice** — answer practice questions
- **Review** — revisit previously covered content
- **Drill** — intensive question repetition on a weak topic
- **Benchmark** — timed mini-exam to assess progress

### Domain scores

Below the daily tasks, a full breakdown of all 12 Series 7 topic domains shows your mastery level for each as a color-coded progress bar. Topics are sorted weakest first so you can see at a glance where to focus.

The 12 domains tracked are: Equity Securities, Debt Securities, Options, Municipal Securities, Government Securities, Investment Companies, Direct Participation, Variable Annuities, Retirement Plans, Business Development, Customer Evaluation, and Customer Instructions.

A trend arrow next to each bar indicates whether your mastery in that domain is improving, declining, or holding steady.

### Milestone progress

If your study plan includes milestones — intermediate goals set to keep you on track toward your target exam date — they appear as a timeline below the domain scores. Each milestone shows its target date, current status (Pending, Completed, Missed, or Deferred), and completion date if applicable.

## Taking a practice exam

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    On the Exam Prep dashboard, scroll to **Practice Exams**. Each template card shows the exam name, description, number of questions, and time limit. Click **Start Practice Exam** on the template you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review exam details and begin">
    A confirmation page shows the question count and time limit. Read the notice: once you click **Begin Exam**, the timer starts immediately and you cannot pause it. The exam auto-submits when time expires. Click **Begin Exam** when you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate and answer questions">
    You can move freely between questions during the exam. Answer every question before the timer runs out or before you manually submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and receive your score">
    After the exam scores, your result appears in **Recent Results** on the Exam Prep dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you have an in-progress exam session (not a diagnostic), a banner appears at the top of the Exam Prep dashboard with a **Continue Exam** button. You can only have one active non-diagnostic session at a time.
</Note>

## Reading your exam results

The **Recent Results** table on the Exam Prep dashboard shows your last five scored sessions. Each row displays:

- The date the exam was submitted
- Your percentage score
- A **Pass** or **Fail** badge (72% is the passing threshold)
- A **Review** link to see the detailed question-by-question breakdown

Click **Review** to see which questions you answered correctly and incorrectly, along with explanations for each answer.

## Readiness score explained

Your **Readiness Score** is a composite metric out of 100 calculated from your diagnostic results and subsequent practice exam performance. The score updates each time you complete a scored session. 

- **Below 50** — red; significant study is needed
- **50–71** — yellow; you're making progress but not yet at passing level
- **72+** — green; you are at or above the passing threshold

<Tip>
  Use the **Quick Start** section at the bottom of the Study Dashboard to jump directly to your primary task, launch a practice quiz, or navigate to your weakest topics for a targeted review session.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I completed the diagnostic but my competency profile isn't showing. What happened?">
    The competency profile only appears after the diagnostic session is fully scored. If you closed the browser before the session was submitted, return to the Exam Prep dashboard — you may see a Resume Diagnostic prompt. If the session is gone, contact your instructor to reset it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I retake the diagnostic?">
    The diagnostic is intended to be taken once to establish your baseline. Subsequent practice exams serve the same purpose for measuring improvement. If you believe your diagnostic result was affected by a technical issue, contact your instructor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My readiness score went down after a practice exam. Is that normal?">
    Yes. Your readiness score incorporates both your diagnostic baseline and all subsequent practice results. A lower-than-usual score on a specific exam can bring the composite score down slightly. Keep studying — the trend over time matters more than any single session.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does 'untested' mean next to a domain in the domain scores?">
    You have not yet answered any questions tagged to that domain, either in the diagnostic or in a practice exam. Take a practice exam or complete a Drill task for that domain to establish a score.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
