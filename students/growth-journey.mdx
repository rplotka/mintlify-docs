Learning In Stride layers a gamification system called the Growth Journey on top of your regular coursework. As you submit assignments on time, earn strong grades, attend class, and participate, the platform awards points, levels up your skills, and unlocks achievement badges. The Growth Journey is separate from your academic grade — it is a way to visualize your progress and stay motivated throughout the semester.

## Points

Points are the core currency of the Growth Journey. They accumulate across all your course activity and feed into the class leaderboard.

### How you earn points

| Activity | Points awarded |
|----------|---------------|
| Submit an assignment on time | +10 pts |
| Earn a perfect score (100%) on an assignment | +25 pts |
| Stay active every day (daily streak bonus) | +5 pts per day |
| Earn an achievement | Varies by achievement |

<Info>
  Points earned in the gamification system do not affect your academic grade. They are tracked independently and used only for the leaderboard and achievements.
</Info>

### Viewing your points

Your total points, weekly points, and monthly points appear in the **Stats Overview** bar at the top of your dashboard. Your current leaderboard rank is shown alongside them.

## Achievements

Achievements are milestones you unlock by meeting specific thresholds in your academic activity. Each achievement has a name, description, point reward, and XP reward. Some achievements are secret — they don't appear in your list until you unlock them.

### Tracking achievement progress

The **Achievements & Rewards** card at the bottom of your dashboard shows:

- How many achievements you've earned
- How many new badges you've received since your last visit
- Your current streak

Click the card to expand it and see your three most recently earned achievements, each with its point value and the date it was earned. Click **View all achievements, badges & XP shop** to go to the full achievements page.

### Achievement categories

Achievements span several categories including academic performance, consistency (streaks), participation, and special milestones. Progress toward incomplete achievements is tracked so you know how close you are to unlocking the next one.

## Badges

Badges are a visual reward system distinct from achievements. Each badge has a tier (such as Bronze, Silver, or Gold), an emoji or icon, and a description of what you did to earn it.

New badges are flagged with a **New** indicator when you visit the achievements page for the first time after earning them. The total number of new badges is also surfaced in the achievements summary card on your dashboard.

## Level and XP

In addition to points, the Growth Journey tracks your **experience points (XP)** and assigns you a level with a title. Your level rises as your XP total crosses successive thresholds. Your current level, title, XP earned, and XP needed for the next level are displayed on the full achievements page.

## Daily streaks

A streak counts how many consecutive days you have been active in Learning In Stride. Staying active means completing at least one scored activity — such as submitting an assignment or completing an exam prep task — each calendar day.

Your current streak and longest streak are shown on the achievements page and in the compact summary on your dashboard. If you use the Series 7 Exam Prep module, a separate study streak is tracked there as well.

<Tip>
  Streaks reset to zero if you miss a day. Log in and complete at least one activity each day to keep your streak alive and collect the daily streak bonus.
</Tip>

## The Growth Journey page

For a deeper view into your progress within a specific course, navigate to the **Growth Journey** page from your course hub.

### Header stats

Four summary numbers appear at the top of the page:

- **Total XP** — all experience points earned in this course
- **Skills** — the number of skills being tracked for you
- **Mastered** — how many skills have reached level 5 (the maximum)
- **Achievements** — total milestones reached in this course

### Overview vs. Skill Tree

Use the toggle buttons to switch between two views:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Overview">
    Shows a **Milestones Carousel** of your earned and upcoming learning milestones, a **Your Skills** grid with up to four skill cards, and an **Anonymous Comparison** card showing how your score compares to the class average and your percentile ranking without revealing any individual classmate's data.

    If Learning In Stride has identified your strengths and growth opportunities through the analytics system, a **Personal Insights** card appears with a list of your strengths and suggested areas to focus on.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Skill Tree">
    Displays your full skill tree organized by category. Each skill node shows your current level, XP progress toward the next level, and trend direction. Skills you haven't started appear as locked nodes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Skill mastery levels

Each tracked skill progresses through levels 1 to 5 based on your assignment scores and engagement with related content. Reaching level 5 in a skill means you've mastered it. The skills tracked are specific to your course content and are defined by your instructor.

### Celebration modal

When you reach a new milestone, a celebration modal appears automatically the next time you visit the Growth Journey page. You can acknowledge each milestone and dismiss the modal. Milestones that haven't been acknowledged yet are tracked until you see them.

## The leaderboard

The class leaderboard ranks all students in your course by total points. Navigate to it from the Grades section or the Growth Journey page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the leaderboard">
    Click **Leaderboard** in the navigation. The leaderboard loads showing the top 20 students.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch time periods">
    Use the **All Time**, **This Month**, and **This Week** tabs to compare yourself across different windows. Weekly and monthly rankings reset at the start of each period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your position">
    Your entry is highlighted in the list regardless of your rank. The display name used is your chosen display name, not your RCS ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Top positions display special icons: a gold crown for first place, a silver medal for second, and a bronze medal for third.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my achievements or leaderboard rank affect my final grade?">
    No. The Growth Journey system is entirely separate from your academic grade. Points, achievements, badges, and leaderboard rank have no impact on any grade calculation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see my Growth Journey page?">
    The Growth Journey page requires a course semester ID. Navigate to it from within a specific course hub, not from the main dashboard. If the page shows an error, try refreshing or check with your instructor that the growth journey feature is enabled for your course.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I earned an achievement but it isn't showing in the list. What do I do?">
    Achievement processing can take a few seconds after the triggering event. Refresh the page or click the refresh icon on the Growth Journey dashboard. If it still doesn't appear after a minute, the achievement criteria may not have been fully met yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the anonymous comparison card?">
    The anonymous comparison shows your course score alongside the class average and your percentile range, without identifying any individual classmate. It is calculated from all students enrolled in the same course section.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
