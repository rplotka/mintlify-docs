The Assignments panel in Learning In Stride consolidates every piece of work from all your enrolled courses into a single, filterable list. You never have to jump between course pages to figure out what's due next — everything is sorted by urgency and updated in real time so you always know where you stand.

## Viewing your assignments

The **All Assignments** panel lives on your student dashboard. If you're enrolled in multiple courses, tabs at the top of the panel let you switch between them. Each assignment shows:

- Its **status badge** (see below)
- The **assignment title** and category
- The **due date or time remaining**
- The **point value** or your score if already graded

Assignments that do not have a due date (such as unscheduled in-class exercises) are intentionally excluded from the list.

## Understanding status indicators

Every assignment has a colored status badge that reflects its current state.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Late">
    The assignment is past its due date and you have not submitted anything. The badge is **red**. Late submissions may still be accepted depending on your instructor's policy — check the assignment page for details.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Due">
    The assignment is due within the next **seven days** and is not yet submitted. The badge is **blue**. These are your immediate priorities.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Scheduled">
    The assignment exists and has a due date, but it is more than seven days away. The badge is **gray**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Submitted">
    You have uploaded your work and it is awaiting grading. The badge is **yellow**. No further action is needed unless your instructor returns it for revision.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Graded">
    Your submission has been graded and the score has been released. The badge is **green** and your score appears next to the title.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Quiz urgency indicators

Quizzes behave differently from regular assignments because they have a fixed open/close window. When a quiz is detected, it is pinned to the top of the list and its row pulses with a colored border:

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Green | Quiz is upcoming — opening more than 48 hours from now |
| Yellow | Quiz opening within 48 hours — prepare soon |
| Orange | Quiz is **live** right now |
| Red | Quiz window has **closed** — submission is no longer possible |

<Note>
  The assignment list refreshes quiz urgency colors every 60 seconds so the display stays accurate without requiring a manual page reload.
</Note>

## Filtering assignments

Use the filter pills above the assignment list to focus on what you need.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter by status">
    Click any of the status pills — **Late**, **Due**, **Scheduled**, **Submitted**, or **Graded** — to show only assignments in that state. You can activate multiple filters at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by category">
    If your course uses assignment categories (such as Labs, Quizzes, or Projects), a second row of category pills appears. Click a category to narrow the list further.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear filters">
    Click **Clear filters** in the panel header to remove all active filters and see the full list again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Submitting an assignment

You can submit from the dashboard header or directly from an individual assignment page.

### Submitting from the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the Submit button">
    In the dashboard header, click **Submit**. A modal opens listing every assignment that still needs a submission, sorted with late items first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your assignment">
    Click the assignment you want to submit. You are taken directly to that assignment's page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    On the assignment page, drag and drop your file or click the upload area to browse. Accepted file types and size limits are shown on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm submission">
    Click **Submit**. The status badge for that assignment changes to **Submitted** immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Submitting from the assignment list

Click any assignment title in the list to open its page. From there you can read the full instructions, review any attached rubric, and upload your work.

<Tip>
  If you need to resubmit before the deadline, open the assignment page again and upload a new file. The previous submission is replaced.
</Tip>

## Checking for grades and feedback

Once your instructor grades your submission, the assignment's status changes from **Submitted** to **Graded** and your score appears in the list. To read your instructor's written feedback:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assignment">
    Click the assignment title in the list to open the full assignment page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to the grade section">
    Your score, letter grade (if applicable), and any written feedback from your instructor appear in the **Grade** section at the bottom of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Grades marked with a clock icon are **preliminary** and may still change. A checkmark icon indicates the grade is finalized.
</Note>

## Late submissions

An assignment with a **Late** badge has passed its due date without a submission. Whether late submissions are accepted is up to your instructor. If late submission is allowed:

1. Click the assignment in the list (or use the **Submit** modal from the dashboard).
2. The assignment page will indicate that the submission is late.
3. Upload your file as normal.

<Info>
  Some courses apply an automatic point deduction for late work. Check the assignment instructions or your course syllabus for the policy.
</Info>

## Testing accommodations

If you have a letter from the Student Accessibility Services (SAS) office granting extended time on timed assessments, you can upload it directly in Learning In Stride to request your accommodation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to your course">
    Go to your course page and find the **Testing Accommodations** link in the course navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your letter">
    Click **Submit Accommodation Letter**, choose a PDF or image of your SAS letter (maximum 10 MB), add an optional note for your instructor, and click **Submit Request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Your request shows as **Pending Review**. Once your instructor approves it, the accommodation is activated automatically for future timed assessments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your accommodation document is stored securely and is only visible to your instructor. No diagnosis information is stored or displayed in the system.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An assignment I know about isn't showing in the list. Why?">
    The assignment list only shows items that have a due date and require a file upload. Assignments without a due date set by your instructor, or items that are attendance-only, are excluded by design.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I submitted an assignment but the status still shows Due. Is it saved?">
    After submitting, the status should update within a few seconds. Try refreshing the page. If the status still shows Due after refreshing, check the assignment page directly to verify your submission is recorded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see assignments from past semesters?">
    The assignment list shows work for your currently enrolled courses. Past-semester grades and submissions are available in the **My Grades** section under each historical course.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
